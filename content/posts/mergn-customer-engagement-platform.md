### **Helping Brands Build Stronger Customer Relationships**

Mergn is a state-of-the-art Customer Engagement Platform designed to help brands accelerate conversions, improve retention, and increase customer lifetime value. By leveraging our experience scaling online businesses and building world-class technology, Mergn has delivered remarkable results for its partnering brands. The platform empowers businesses to build meaningful customer relationships through:

* **Targeted Segments**: Identity and group customers based on behavior, preferences, and demographics.
    
* **Campaigns and Journeys**: Design tailored customer journeys specific to each customer across Email, SMS, Web Popups, Push Notifications, WhatsApp, RoboCall, and Content Cards.
    
* **Multi-Channel Engagement**: Ensure seamless communication through multiple customer touchpoints.

### **My Role in This Journey**

As a Full Stack Engineer, I contributed significantly to Mergn’s success by:

* **Development and Maintenance**: Built, tested, and documented features using technologies like React, Next.js (14+ App Router), NextUI, Tailwind, TypeScript, Nx Microservices, NestJS, PostgreSQL, and AWS.
    
* **Optimized Development**: Reduced time-to-market by 40% with a rapid MVP launch and improved development efficiency by 60% through standardized practices.
    
* **Team Leadership**: Led a team of developers on Frontend to ensure clean code architecture, TDD, CI/CD pipelines, and full-stack optimizations.
    
* **Shopify App & SDK Development**: Single-handedly developed the Mergn Shopify App and TypeScript Web SDK, handling 10M+ monthly traffic across 10+ major brands like Domino’s Pizza, Broadway Pizza, Naheed.pk, and more. [Explore the NPM SDK](https://www.npmjs.com/package/mergn-webapp-sdk) | [Check the Shopify App](https://apps.shopify.com/mergn)
    
* **Feature Implementation**:
    
    * Anonymous user ID sync
        
    * Event and attribute tracking
        
    * Web popups and push notifications
        
    * Content cards and targeted campaigns (boosted engagement by 60% and conversions by 75%).
        
    * Notify Customers of Stock Items when In Stock.
        
* **Campaign Builder**: Developed a 7+ channel Campaign and Journey Builder, reducing user time by 50% and accelerating campaign rollouts.
    
* **Scalable Authentication**: Implemented secure multi-tenant authentication with AWS Cognito, NextAuth, and Next.js middleware, supporting 50+ users across 10+ tenants.
    

### **Key Results**

* **4x Engagement**: Personalized tags and targeted campaigns significantly boosted customer interactions.
    
* **75 to 400% increase in Conversions**: Advanced SDK features led to a measurable increase in conversions.
    
* **50% Faster Rollouts & Easy UX**: The multi-channel Campaign & Journey Builder streamlined processes, saving users time, and allowing non-technical users to build complex segments, and logical conditions.
    

Mergn exemplifies how technology and thoughtful engineering can redefine customer engagement, setting a benchmark for innovation in the industry.
